01 / About

From the paint booth to the browser

I spent the first stretch of my career on the factory floor — running robotic paint cells at Motherson Automotive, holding process quality on Mahindra & Mahindra's paint shop line, and later closing out PTCED work at Tata Motors. That work taught me to read a spec sheet the way most people read a sentence: tolerances, sequencing, what happens if one station falls out of sync.

I'm now finishing an MCA at Invertis University and building full-stack products — React and React Native on the front end, Node/Express and MongoDB or MySQL underneath. The habit hasn't changed: I still care more about whether a system holds up under real conditions than whether the demo looks good.
